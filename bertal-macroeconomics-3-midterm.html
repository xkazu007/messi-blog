Bertal's Macroeconomics 3 midterm

MidtermsMacroeconomics 28 Oct 2026

Midterm week has a look: a laptop open, notes somewhere under the elbow, a bottle of water, earphones for the walk home. This one belongs to Prof. Bertal's Macroeconomics 3 course — the midterm his students are sitting.

The midterm

Macro 3 is the kind of course where the exam is won in the days before it. The models only click after you have worked through them yourself — read once, then close the book and do the problem again from scratch. That is what this week is for.

There is no shortcut around it: the curves, the mechanisms, the "what happens when this moves" questions. You either see the chain of effects or you don't, and the only way to see it is repetition.

The kit

  • The laptop, open and actually being used.
  • A bottle of water — hydration is a study strategy too.
  • Earphones, for the walk home and the last review.
  • Notes, somewhere under the elbow.
